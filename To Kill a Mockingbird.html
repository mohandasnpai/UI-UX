To Kill a Mockingbird

Auther: Harper Lee

Genre: Novel, Bildungsroman, Southern Gothic, Southern Fiction, Thriller, Domestic Fiction, Legal Story

Description:To Kill a Mockingbird is a classic 1960 novel by Harper Lee about racial injustice, moral growth, and the loss of innocence in the American South. Set during the Great Depression in Maycomb, Alabama, the story is told through the eyes of a young girl named Scout Finch.
